# Product Positioning

DisciplineOS is an open-source, data-source-agnostic personal investment discipline system that helps investors generate, enforce, audit, and review their own investment rules.

## What It Is

- 投资者纪律画像系统。
- 标的纪律卡生成器。
- 交易前决策闸门。
- 仓位治理与违规账本。
- 结构化月度复盘工具。

## What It Is Not

- 不是荐股系统。
- 不是自动交易机器人。
- 不是量化回测平台。
- 不是普通交易流水账。
- 不是以 AI 为最终裁判的投资顾问。

## Product Soul

Most investment tools track what you own. DisciplineOS governs how you decide.
